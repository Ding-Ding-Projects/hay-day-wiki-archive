# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We welcome contributors who are respectful, patient, and precise. Discussions should focus on the source material, the reader, the implementation, and evidence. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose another person's private information.

## Project-specific expectations

- Respect source authors and license requirements.
- Do not submit credentials, private data, or unlicensed media.
- Do not misrepresent planning counts or incomplete features as shipped.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and leave room for correction.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove material that violates this code, and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or source rights. Report concerns through the repository's private security or conduct channel when available.
